This week many of you observed how people use public spaces – here are a few patterns from your notes.

===

### What Came Up

* People rarely use a space the way its designers intended
* Small obstacles, such as a missing sign or a hard-to-find entrance, change behaviour more than you might expect
* What people say they do and what they actually do are often different

Keep these patterns in mind as you plan your own user research in [Module 8](/modules/module-08).
