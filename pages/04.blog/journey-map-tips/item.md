The [Journey Map](https://canvas.sfu.ca/courses/55288/assignments) assignment is due **Tue Jun 12 11:59pm PT** – here are a few tips to help.

===

### Tips

* Base every stage of your map on what you learned from real people
* Show feelings, not just steps – the highs and lows are what make a journey map useful
* Mark the moments where a better design could make the biggest difference
* Keep your map readable at a glance
